## Interpretation and final contract

The review distinguishes exact equivalence on reachable triangular bases,
redundant guards, performance-only work, floating-point threshold differences,
malformed-input gaps, incomplete reflection searches, and missing assertions.
Passing tests alone is never the reason for an equivalence decision.

Scientific contract changes belong to #39: strict unsupported-input diagnostics,
neutral-atom form factors, explicit incomplete searches, 10⁻⁷-degree degenerate
grouping and deterministic first-family labels replace several old assumptions.
The old 0.025-degree grouping and strongest-versus-accumulated label decisions
are retired explicitly, not silently called equivalent. New tests also target
coordinate-key collisions and the inclusive upper profile endpoint.

Some exact degeneracy/intensity threshold equalities remain distinguishable in
principle but are outside the independently validated well-conditioned reference
set or below its declared numerical tolerances. Their IDs and reasoning remain
visible; this work does not claim a 100% mutation score, complete numerical proof,
or experimental intensity accuracy. Finite capped searches remain incomplete
even if their tests pass. See the [scientific contract](scientific-validity.md).

The final model's mutation measurement uses the same toolchain but targets
`pxrd.ts` and the new `scattering.ts`; it has a different denominator and must not
be compared as a score improvement over the unchanged-source run. Its raw output
is `reports/scientific/mutation-current.{json,html}`.
